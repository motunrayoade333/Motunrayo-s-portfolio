Maven Toys Mexico — SQL Capstone

SQL Server (SSMS)

A four-phase SQL Server capstone on the Mexico Toy Sales (Maven Toys) dataset — a star schema of sales, products, stores, and inventory covering January 2022 to December 2023. The project moves from raw data cleaning through exploratory analysis, advanced business questions using CTEs and window functions, and finally into reusable views, stored procedures, and summary tables for ongoing reporting.

WITH CATEGORY_AVG AS ( SELECT PRODUCT_CATEGORY, AVG((UNITS * PRODUCT_PRICE) - (UNITS * PRODUCT_COST)) AS AVG_CATEGORY_PROFIT FROM PRODUCTS JOIN SALES ON PRODUCTS.PRODUCT_ID = SALES.PRODUCT_ID GROUP BY PRODUCT_CATEGORY ) SELECT ST.STORE_NAME, P.PRODUCT_CATEGORY, SUM((UNITS * PRODUCT_PRICE) - (UNITS * PRODUCT_COST)) AS STORE_CATEGORY_PROFIT, CA.AVG_CATEGORY_PROFIT, CASE WHEN SUM((UNITS*PRODUCT_PRICE)-(UNITS*PRODUCT_COST)) > CA.AVG_CATEGORY_PROFIT THEN 'ABOVE AVERAGE' WHEN SUM((UNITS*PRODUCT_PRICE)-(UNITS*PRODUCT_COST)) < CA.AVG_CATEGORY_PROFIT THEN 'BELOW AVERAGE' ELSE 'AT AVERAGE' END AS PERFORMANCE FROM SALES S JOIN PRODUCTS P ON S.PRODUCT_ID = P.PRODUCT_ID JOIN STORES ST ON S.STORE_ID = ST.STORE_ID JOIN CATEGORY_AVG CA ON P.PRODUCT_CATEGORY = CA.PRODUCT_CATEGORY GROUP BY ST.STORE_NAME, P.PRODUCT_CATEGORY, CA.AVG_CATEGORY_PROFIT ORDER BY P.PRODUCT_CATEGORY, STORE_CATEGORY_PROFIT DESC;

Key Findings

  • Several stores fall below their category profit average, particularly in Electronics and Toys — worth investigating pricing strategy and stock availability there.
  • High-revenue stores carrying fewer than 5 units on best-sellers face direct stockout risk — the sp_inventory_alert procedure flags these automatically.
  • The top 3 products in each category generate a disproportionately large share of category revenue — a case for prioritizing their stock and shelf placement.
  • Most categories grew from 2022 to 2023; Art & Crafts grew strongest, Electronics slowest — suggesting where to shift investment.
  • High-margin products are frequently not the highest-volume sellers — a missed revenue opportunity worth targeted promotion.
